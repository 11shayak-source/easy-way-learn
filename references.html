The Asiatic Society

PAPER PRESENTATION

Kalyan Bhattacharya’s research and innovative pedagogical frameworks were formally presented at The Asiatic Society. Founded in 1784 by the eminent philologist Sir William Jones, The Asiatic Society stands at 1 Park Street, Kolkata, as the premier institution for Oriental research and the preservation of rare manuscripts and antiquities.

To be invited to present a scholarly paper at an institution that has driven scientific and historical inquiry in Asia for over two centuries serves as an absolute testament to the profound academic weight underlying the Easy Way Learn modules.

Why This Matters:

  • ✓ Validates our study materials against the highest standards of Indian academia.
  • ✓ Connects modern digital learning with a legacy of deep literary research.

Bardhaman Science Centre

GUEST LECTURE & SEMINAR

Further establishing our commitment to analytical development, Kalyan Bhattacharya delivered a highly acclaimed lecture at the Bardhaman Science Centre. Operating under the National Council of Science Museums (NCSM), Ministry of Culture, Govt. of India, this district-level center has been communicating science since 1994.

The Science Club framework initiated by Easy Way Learn directly aligns with the center's mission of fostering hands-on practical experiments and preparing young innovators for advanced scientific pursuits.

The Science Club Connection:

  • ✓ Demonstrates a proven track record in practical physics, mathematics, and electronics education.
  • ✓ Recognized by governmental-level science communicators.
