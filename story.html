The Genesis

Of Easy Way Learn

Authored by Kalyan Bhattacharya

Chapter I: Let Me Introduce Myself

Education is not merely the transfer of information; it is the ignition of absolute potential. As the visionary behind Easy Way Learn, my goal has been to construct a platform that transcends the rigid boundaries of traditional academia.

My journey did not begin with a desire to build a standard corporation, but rather from a profound observation of the educational divide. While metropolitan students had access to structured, analytical learning, eager minds across the districts were left navigating an archaic system.

Earning my M.Sc. in Engineering from the UK laid the critical foundation for analytical problem-solving. This international exposure led to my roles as an Ex. CEO and Board Member in the UK, as well as active memberships with the International Project Management Association (IPMA) World, British Council Alumni UK, and the Commonwealth Foundation.

These international standardizations were the exact blueprints I needed. I returned with a singular vision: to democratize elite education and bring world-class analytical frameworks to every single student, regardless of their geographical constraints.

Chapter II: The Two Core Wings

To build an empire of knowledge, one must establish an unbreakable foundation. Thus, the Trust was divided into two Core Wings: Education and the Science Club.

The Education wing focuses entirely on the Easy Learning English framework. It is a highly structured, interactive model designed to make mastering the English language intuitive. We bridge the gap between basic grammar and high-level academic writing, preparing students for global exams like IELTS and TOEFL.

However, language is only the vessel. The substance is pure analytical thought. This birthed the Science Club Initiative. Fostering analytical thinking, practical experiments, and theoretical sciences, the Science Club is meticulously engineered to prepare students for advanced academic pursuits, including IIT and UPSC/IPS preparation.

"We do not just teach students to memorize formulas; we teach them how to engineer solutions."
